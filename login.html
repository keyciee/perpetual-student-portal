Institutional Data Privacy Notice

Compliance Statement: In adherence to Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012) and the National Privacy Commission (NPC), the University of Perpetual Help System DALTA – Molino Campus is committed to protecting your personal data privacy.

1. Scope of Information Processed:

  • Student Profile (Full Name, Student ID, Institutional Email)
  • Academic Performance (Course units, grades, attendance, curriculum standing)
  • Financial Ledgers (Tuition billing statements, payment channels, transaction IDs)

2. Institutional Purpose:

Collected records are processed exclusively for official academic evaluation, semester enrollment verification, cashierless tuition settlement, and RFID student smart card authorization.

3. Data Confidentiality & Security:

All sensitive transactions are encrypted and stored within verified session scopes. No personal educational information is shared with unauthorized third parties or commercial entities.

4. Student Rights:

Students have the right to access, inspect, and request correction of inaccurate institutional records through the Campus Registrar and Data Protection Office.
